DiceGameJS
JavaScript browser game where two players take turns rolling a dice to reach 100 points. The first player to score 100 wins!

Features:
Roll a virtual dice to accumulate points
Hold your score to secure your turn’s points
Automatic player switching on rolling a 1
Winner detection when score reaches 100
New Game button to reset and play again

Technologies Used:
Language: JavaScript
UI & Styling: HTML, CSS
Environment: Browser-based (no frameworks)

Getting Started:
Clone or download this repository.
Open the index.html file in any web browser.
Play the game by clicking Roll Dice, Hold, or New Game.

How to Play:
Players take turns.
Click Roll Dice to get a random number (1 to 6).
If the dice shows 1, you lose your turn and score nothing.
If it’s 2–6, it adds to your current turn score.
Click Hold to add your current score to your total and end your turn.
First to reach 100 total points wins the game!
